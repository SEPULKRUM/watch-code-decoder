SEPULKRUM

Watch Code Decoder

Independent reference tool for production and batch codes found on Casio watches. Results distinguish documented observations from still-unknown fields.

Currently supports verified portions of 6-character and 11-character codes. Legacy 8-character research is listed separately until the decoding rules are verified.
